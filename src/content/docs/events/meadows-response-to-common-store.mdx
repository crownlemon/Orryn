## Account

Ser Robyn Rhysling brought a request from Ser Garth Tyrell and the Lord Seneschal for Reach lords to declare their grain, fodder and carts and contribute one tenth of their grain to common depots. Lord Alaric agreed to disclose and allow inspection of his stores but refused to send grain outside Grassy Vale. He offered to sell any surplus at a fair price once his own domain was fed.

Participants: [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/), [Ser Robyn Rhysling](/characters/robyn-rhysling/).

Location: [Grassfield Keep](/places/grassfield-keep/).

## Consequences

Alaric distinguished his earlier gift of eighteen sacks from any obligation to contribute further. No general settlement of the Reach-wide proposal is established by this exchange.
