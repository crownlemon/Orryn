**Suppression of the Vulture King** occurred in 206 AC.

## Account

The marcher lords Caron and Dondarrion suppressed the Vulture King's rising in the Red Mountains.

Recorded participants: [Lord Pearse Caron](/characters/pearse-caron/), [Lord Dondarrion (given name unrecorded)](/characters/dondarrion-unnamed-lord/).
