**Battle of the Honeywine** forms part of [Dance of the Dragons](/events/dance-of-the-dragons/).

## Account

The Reach divided in the Dance: Ormund Hightower opposed forces led by Rowan, Tarly, Beesbury and Costayne. The precise year varies in the published chronology.

## Background

During the Dance of the Dragons.

## Consequences

The event forms part of the historical record preceding the present campaign.
