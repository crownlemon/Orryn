The rebellion of 196 AC pitted supporters of Daeron II against those of Daemon Blackfyre.

## Account

Daemon's bid for the throne ended in defeat at the Redgrass Field. Its aftermath continued to shape the loyalties of houses across the Reach and Stormlands.
