The **Blackfyre Rebellion** was the civil war of 196 AC between King Daeron II and the supporters of his half-brother Daemon.

## Account

The contest ended at the [Battle of the Redgrass Field](/events/battle-redgrass-field/). Daemon and his elder twin sons died, and Aegor Rivers accompanied the surviving Blackfyres into exile.

## Background

Aegon IV's legitimisation of his natural children and the prestige of the sword Blackfyre gave Daemon's supporters an alternative dynastic claim. The accusation against Daeron's parentage was a political allegation, not established parentage.

## Consequences

Daeron remained king. Confiscations and hostages helped shape the postwar settlement, which continues to influence houses in the Orryn campaign.
