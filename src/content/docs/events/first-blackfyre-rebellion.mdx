The **Blackfyre Rebellion** of 196 AC challenged the rule of Daeron II.

## Account

The rebellion ended in a royal victory at the Redgrass Field. Daemon and his elder twin sons died; his surviving family fled Westeros.

## Background

The legitimisation of Aegon IV's natural children and disputed royal succession helped create a competing dynastic cause. Accusations about Daeron's birth are not treated as proven.

## Consequences

The postwar settlement of lands and hostages shapes the campaign's politics.
