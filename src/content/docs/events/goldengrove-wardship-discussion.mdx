## Account

Lord Alaric Meadows visited Lord Rycherd Rowan at Goldengrove to establish the terms of Lord Harys Oakheart's wardship. Rowan said Lord Leo Tyrell had entrusted the boy to him, that he would remain until grown and knighted, and that Leo retained authority over the boy's marriage. Both lords objected to the manner in which Lady Arlanna's proposed match with Elyce Meadows had been communicated.

Participants: [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/), [Lord Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/).

Location: [Goldengrove](/places/goldengrove/).

## Consequences

Alaric said he would not accept a betrothal while Harys was a hostage and would require Lord Leo's own decision. Rowan sought Alaric's support in a future audience at Highgarden. A proposed audience or marriage is not treated as a concluded agreement.

*Source: [Chapter 66](/chapters/chapter-66/), Chapter 66 — Whose Man.*
