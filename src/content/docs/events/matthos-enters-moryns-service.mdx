## Account

At Highgarden, Lord Alaric Meadows confirmed that his son Matthos would enter Ser Moryn Tyrell's service as squire. Ser Olyvar had offered a place in his own company, but Alaric chose to honour his earlier approach to Moryn. Moryn accepted and undertook to provide training and attendance within his household.

Participants: [Matthos Meadows](/characters/matthos-meadows/), [Ser Moryn Tyrell](/characters/moryn-tyrell/), [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/), [Ser Olyvar Tyrell](/characters/olyvar-tyrell/).

Location: [Highgarden](/places/highgarden/).

## Consequences

Matthos remained at Highgarden rather than returning to Grassfield. The decision did not amount to a formal declaration of political allegiance.
