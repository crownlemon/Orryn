## Account

Travelling west in Lord Alaric Meadows's cart, Orryn Grandison was stopped at Sedgeford by Ser Osmund Sedgley. Osmund required the toll applicable to Reach traffic. Orryn invoked Alaric's name, but the demand stood. The party paid two silver stags and three pennies before crossing the ford.

Participants: [Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/), [Ser Osmund Sedgley](/characters/osmund-sedgley/).

Location: [Sedgeford](/places/sedgeford/).

## Consequences

The toll was entered in the local book. The payment settled this crossing; it did not establish a general exemption or change the ford's jurisdiction.
