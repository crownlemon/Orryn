**Last Storm** forms part of [Aegon's Conquest](/events/aegons-conquest/).

## Account

The defeat of Argilac ended the independent Storm Kingdom. Argella's subsequent marriage to Orys established House Baratheon.

## Background

Around 2 BC, during Aegon's Conquest.

## Consequences

The event forms part of the historical record preceding the present campaign.
