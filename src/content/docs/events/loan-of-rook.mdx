## Account

At Sedgeford, Orryn accepted the offer of a dun gelding conveyed by Ser Jon Penrose on Lady Alysanne's behalf. The clerk recorded the seven-year-old horse, named Rook, as lent from the Sedgeford stable for Orryn to ride west. Orryn sent his thanks to Lady Alysanne through Ser Jon.

Participants: [Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/), [Ser Jon Penrose](/characters/jon-penrose/), [Lady Alysanne Penrose](/characters/alysanne-penrose/), [Ser Osmund Sedgley](/characters/osmund-sedgley/).

Location: [Sedgeford](/places/sedgeford/).

## Consequences

Orryn continued on horseback. The written record describes a loan, not an outright transfer of ownership.
