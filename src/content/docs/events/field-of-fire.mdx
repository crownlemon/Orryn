**Field of Fire** forms part of [Aegon's Conquest](/events/aegons-conquest/).

## Account

The Gardener and Lannister kings opposed the Targaryen conquest. Mern IX's death ended the Gardener monarchy; Highgarden subsequently passed to the Tyrells.

## Background

Around 2 BC, during Aegon's Conquest.

## Consequences

The event forms part of the historical record preceding the present campaign.
