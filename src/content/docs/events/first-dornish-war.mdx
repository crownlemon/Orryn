**First Dornish War** lasted from 4 to 13 AC.

## Account

Aegon I's attempt to bring Dorne under his sovereignty ended with a negotiated peace in 13 AC. Dorne retained its independence.

Recorded participants: [King Aegon I Targaryen](/characters/aegon-i-targaryen/), [Queen Rhaenys Targaryen](/characters/rhaenys-targaryen-conqueror/).
