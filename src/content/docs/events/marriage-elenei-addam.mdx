## Account

Elenei Grandison married Ser Addam Penrose in the sept at Grandview, with Septon Hollis officiating. The Penrose party included Addam's elder brother Ser Jon and their mother Lady Alysanne. Orryn Grandison attended the wedding before leaving for his service in the Reach.

Participants: [Lady Elenei Penrose](/characters/elenei-grandison/), [Ser Addam Penrose](/characters/addam-penrose/), [Ser Jon Penrose](/characters/jon-penrose/), [Lady Alysanne Penrose](/characters/alysanne-penrose/), [Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/).

Location: [Grandview](/places/grandview/).

## Consequences

The marriage joined the Grandison and Penrose families. Elenei and Addam's established marriage is recorded separately in the family graph.
