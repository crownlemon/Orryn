**Battle of the Redgrass Field** occurred in 196 AC.

## Account

The royal army defeated the Blackfyre cause in 196 AC. Daemon and his two eldest sons died; surviving members of his family departed into exile. The battle established Daeron II's continued rule.

## Consequences

The event belongs to the established pre-story history.
