**Battle of the Burning Mill** forms part of the historical record.

## Account

The Blackwoods and Brackens supported rival claimants. Samwell and Amos died; Alysanne was said to have shot Amos.

## Background

129 AC.

## Consequences

Recorded participants: [Lord Samwell Blackwood](/characters/samwell-blackwood-dance/), [Lady Alysanne Blackwood](/characters/alysanne-blackwood/), [Ser Amos Bracken](/characters/amos-bracken/).
