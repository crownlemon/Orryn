This development-only atlas reads the **208 AC house and holding register** from the structured knowledge model. It is organized by the Worldbook's own political tiers, which should not be confused with a rank system established by A Wiki of Ice and Fire.

The map areas and geographic descriptions are preserved in the source inventory, while house names, seats and immediate overlords are connected in the database. Entries without published articles remain plain text rather than broken links.
