The *Town and estate* group records officials and local people associated with Grassy Vale. It does not imply they reside inside Grassfield Keep or are members of Lord Alaric's domestic household. Likewise, recurring visitors are not counted as resident officers.

Matthos Meadows serves Ser Moryn Tyrell at Highgarden, and Leonette married Ser Garlan Footly in Chapter 60. They appear under the family section as relatives, not as evidence of residence at Grassfield Keep.

Only identities and roles documented in Householdbook v12 are included. Roles, names and the exact sourcing line are maintained in the data records; no servants or officers are invented to fill empty positions.
