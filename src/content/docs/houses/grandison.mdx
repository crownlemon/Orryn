**House Grandison of [Grandview](/places/grandview/)** is an old noble house of the western Stormlands, sworn directly to the Baratheons of Storm's End. Its arms show a black sleeping lion on a golden field, and its words are **Rouse Me Not**. The present head of the house is **[Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/)**.

## History

The Grandisons hold an upland lordship near the border with the Reach, with wooded valleys, pasture, horse-breeding country and lands worked for oats, barley and wool. The household has traditionally been associated with hunting, horsemen and dependable service in the Stormlands. Its resources are respectable rather than vast, and the castle stands closer in wealth and power to the region's established lesser lordships than to Storm's End.

An earlier **Lord Lorent Grandison** served among the regents appointed for King Aegon III in 136 AC. His descendants continued to hold Grandview in the centuries that followed.

During the **First Blackfyre Rebellion** of 196 AC, the late **[Lord Gawen Grandison](/characters/gawen-grandison/)** delayed his muster after the call from Storm's End. For nine days his men waited at Grandview, and they rode only after the struggle had been decided at Redgrass Field. Two messages had been sent seeking an answer from Storm's End, but none reached Grandview in time. The incident left the house with a reputation for excessive caution. Among its own household, the delay continued to divide opinion over Lord Gawen's prudence, his obligations and his reluctance to decide without his liege lord.

Lord Gawen was succeeded by his eldest son, Alester. Alester governs the castle with **Lady Lynesse Hertson**, while his younger brother **Ser Steffon** maintains a separate household on family lands nearby. Their differing approaches to money, obligations and the management of Grandview have not always brought them into agreement.

## Family

### Family tree

Lord Gawen's children were **Alester**, **Steffon**, **Raymont** and **Alys**. Alester and Lynesse's children are **[Arlan](/characters/arlan-grandison/)**, heir to Grandview, and **[Elenei](/characters/elenei-grandison/)**, who married Ser Addam Penrose at Grandview. Steffon and **[Maris Gower](/characters/maris-grandison/)** are the parents of **[Alyssa](/characters/alyssa-grandison/)** and **[Orryn](/characters/orryn-grandison/)**. Raymont serves House Baratheon at Storm's End; Alys married into House Musgood.

The marriage of Lady Ellyn Meadows to Lord Gawen forms an enduring connection between Grandview and **[House Meadows](/houses/meadows/)** of Grassy Vale. Their grandson Orryn entered the service of his kinsman **[Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/)** as a squire.
