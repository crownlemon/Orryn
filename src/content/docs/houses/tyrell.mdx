**House Tyrell of Highgarden** is the ruling house of the Reach, owing allegiance to the Iron Throne. Its words are *Growing Strong*. The Tyrells govern an extensive and wealthy region in which ancient landed houses, river lords, merchants, and maritime families retain considerable local authority.

## History

In the reign of King Daeron II, Highgarden is held by **Lord Leo Tyrell**, called the Longthorn, a celebrated knight and jouster. During the First Blackfyre Rebellion in 196 AC, Leo supported Daeron and won victories in the Reach. His force did not reach the Redgrass Field in time for the decisive battle, having failed to assemble promptly.

The settlement after the rebellion altered the balance of noble power across the Reach. Houses that had supported Daemon Blackfyre faced penalties, while other families gained offices or renewed influence. Lord Leo's authority rests partly upon the splendour of Highgarden's court and his ability to conciliate older, proud houses, but his sons and officers perform much of the daily government.

## The ruling family

Leo is married to **Lady Arlanna**, born of House Peake. Their five living children are Ser Garth, Ser Moryn, Lady Ceryse, Ser Olyvar and Lady Brenna, now Lady Caswell.

**Ser Garth Tyrell** is heir to Highgarden, married to Lady Alys of [House Redwyne](/houses/redwyne/). Their eldest son, Ser Harlen, follows him in the succession. Ser Moryn is married to Lady Melessa Ashford, younger sister of Lord Ashford. Ser Olyvar's wife is Lady Lenora Hewett of Oakenshield. Lady Ceryse, widow of a former Lord Oakheart, acts as regent of Old Oak for her young son Lord Harys. Lady Brenna is the second wife of Lord Armond Caswell of Bitterbridge.

## Highgarden and its government

Lord Leo remains the ultimate authority at Highgarden. His sons enjoy influence, but their overlapping circles of associates are not separate governments or armies. Garth works closely with administrative officers and his Redwyne relations; Moryn has ties to Rowan, Peake and Ashford; and Olyvar is prominent among knights and maritime families. Each associated house pursues its own interests.

Lady Arlanna oversees domestic appointments, ceremonial precedence and the hospitality of the court. Ser Arron Redwyne, brother of Lady Alys, serves as Lord Seneschal of Highgarden by Leo's appointment.
