**House Tully** is the ruling dynasty of [The Riverlands](/realm/riverlands/) in the historical baseline.

## History

After the fall of House Hoare, Aegon granted the Tullys authority over the riverlands.

## Family

The 208 AC ruling family has not yet been reconstructed from dated evidence.
