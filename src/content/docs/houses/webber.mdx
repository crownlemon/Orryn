House Webber holds Coldmoat on the Chequy Water, owing allegiance to House Rowan.

## Family

Lord Wyman's only living child and heir is Lady Rohanne, married to Ser Simon Staunton. Ser Wendell Webber, Wyman's cousin, is married to Lady Mina Rowan and serves at Goldengrove.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree houseId="house:webber" />

## Lordship and household

The household at Coldmoat includes Ser Lucas Inchfield, its castellan. The estate neighbours Standfast, and House Inchfield is sworn to Webber in the campaign register.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 3644–3660; Worldbook v10, regional register.*
