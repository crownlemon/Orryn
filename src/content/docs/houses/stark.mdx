**House Stark** is the ruling dynasty of [The North](/realm/north/) in the historical baseline.

## History

The Stark dynasty surrendered its former kingship to Aegon I and retained regional authority under the new monarchy.

## Family

The 208 AC ruling family has not yet been reconstructed from dated evidence.
