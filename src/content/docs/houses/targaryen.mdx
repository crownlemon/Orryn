**House Targaryen** rules the Seven Kingdoms from King's Landing. The royal family also maintains seats at Dragonstone and Summerhall.

## History

The dynasty came from Valyria and established itself on Dragonstone before the Doom. Aegon I founded the monarchy; the Dance of the Dragons weakened the dynasty and the settlement with Dorne under Daeron II completed the political union. The rebellion of 196 AC challenged but did not displace the reigning king.

## Family

The king is [King Daeron II Targaryen](/characters/daeron-ii-targaryen/), married to [Queen Myriah Martell](/characters/myriah-martell/). Their sons are [Prince Baelor Targaryen](/characters/baelor-breakspear/), [Prince Aerys Targaryen](/characters/aerys-targaryen/), [Prince Rhaegel Targaryen](/characters/rhaegel-targaryen/), [Prince Maekar Targaryen](/characters/maekar-targaryen/).

The earlier line of Prince Daemon links Princess Elaena to Princess Rhaena of the Hightower marriage. The established campaign descendants connect the royal family to Penrose and Rowan. The Blackfyres descend from Aegon IV through Daemon Blackfyre.

## Lordship and household

The royal seat is [King's Landing](/places/kings-landing/); Dragonstone is associated with the heir, and Summerhall with Prince Maekar. See [King of the Seven Kingdoms](/titles/king-of-the-seven-kingdoms/) for the dated succession.
