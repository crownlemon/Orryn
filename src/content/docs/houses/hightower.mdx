House Hightower of Oldtown is a great lordly house of the Reach, sworn to Highgarden.

## Family

Lord Jon Hightower and Lady Corenna Costayne have three children: Janna, Abelar and Lynora. Abelar and Lady Daenora Velaryon have a son, Quenton. The separately documented descendants of Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen include Aelora, Rosamund, Clarice and Alysanne; two daughters are not named in the source. The proposed ancestry of Lord Jon has not been promoted to an established fact.

## Lordship and household

Oldtown is a major seat of power at the mouth of the Honeywine. Jon formerly served as Hand under Aegon IV. His present family has marriages with Blackbar, Costayne, Velaryon and Bulwer, distinct from the Garmund–Rhaena branch.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 3073–3085; Worldbook v10.*
