House Ashford holds the castle and market town of Ashford on the Cockleswent.

## Family

Lord Ashford and his wife, born Caron, have three recorded children: Ser Androw, Ser Robert and Lady Gwin, whose given name follows the campaign's adaptation usage. Androw is married to Lady Wylla Merryweather. The lord's sister Melessa is married to Ser Moryn Tyrell.

## Lordship and household

The holding is sworn to Highgarden. Its crossing and market give the lordship regional importance; the later tournament is not an event of the current snapshot.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 2192–2209; Worldbook v10.*
