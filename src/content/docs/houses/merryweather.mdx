House Merryweather holds Longtable at the confluence of the Mander and Blueburn.

## Family

Lord Merryweather and Lady Odessa have three recorded children: Ser Lymond, heir to Longtable; Ser Alyn, now a knight at Goldengrove; and Lady Wylla, married to Ser Androw Ashford.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree houseId="house:merryweather" />

## Lordship and household

Longtable is sworn to Highgarden. The house has a history of royal service, including Lord Marq Merryweather's brief regency for Aegon III in 136 AC.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 2312–2326; Worldbook v10.*
