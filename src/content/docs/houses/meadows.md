**House Meadows of Grassy Vale** is a noble house of the Reach sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Its seat is **[Grassfield Keep](/places/grassfield-keep/)** above the market town of **[Grassy Vale](/places/grassy-vale/)**, near the border with the Stormlands. The house's arms are a green field within a border of flowers of many colours and varieties. In 208 AC, the house is ruled by **[Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/)**.

## History

The history of the Meadows family includes military service to the Iron Throne. During the Faith Militant uprising in **42 AC**, King Maegor I entrusted a Lord Meadows with command of six lordly hosts against the Poor Fellows at Stonebridge. The royalists caught the Poor Fellows attempting to cross the Mander and defeated them, but Lord Meadows was among the knights killed by **Wat the Hewer**.

In later generations, the Meadows held a prosperous lordship whose strength came from the productive lands of the Blueburn valley. Grassy Vale's markets, river crossing, mills and farmlands supported a larger and more commercially active household than many of the neighbouring Stormlander estates. Alongside its own knights and officers, the house received the service of landed knightly families in the surrounding countryside.

One branch of the family became connected to the Grandisons of Grandview when **Lady Ellyn Meadows**, aunt of Lord Alaric, married Lord Gawen Grandison. The houses have retained close ties across the Reach–Stormlands border. **[Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/)**, the youngest son of Alaric's cousin Ser Steffon, serves as Alaric's squire.

Lord Alaric inherited Grassfield Keep after the First Blackfyre Rebellion, during which he had served King Daeron II. He maintains an exacting interest in the estate's revenues, stores, husbandry and obligations. His wife, **[Lady Jeyne Caswell](/characters/jeyne-meadows/)**, links Grassfield to the influential Caswells of Bitterbridge and, through her stepmother Lady Brenna Tyrell, to Highgarden.

## Events in 208 AC

The Meadows household entered into several new relationships during the year. **[Matthos Meadows](/characters/matthos-meadows/)**, Lord Alaric's son and heir, became a squire to Ser Moryn Tyrell at Highgarden. **[Lady Leonette Meadows](/characters/leonette-meadows/)** married Ser Garlan Footly, heir to Tumbleton, at Grassfield Keep and departed to her husband's family. Lady Jeyne was also expecting another child.

A proposed marriage between **[Lady Elyce Meadows](/characters/elyce-meadows/)** and the young Lord Harys Oakheart brought Alaric into negotiations concerning the boy's wardship at Goldengrove. Alaric travelled to the castle of Lord Rycherd Rowan to discuss the matter, insisting that the families and the boy's guardian should be heard before a match was agreed. No betrothal had been concluded.

Lord Alaric is the son of a former Lord Meadows and nephew of Lady Ellyn Grandison, née Meadows. He and Lady Jeyne have three children: **[Leonette](/characters/leonette-meadows/)**, **[Matthos](/characters/matthos-meadows/)** and **[Elyce](/characters/elyce-meadows/)**.

## Sworn houses

- House Bushy of Hedgefort
- House Dunmere of Dunmere Hall
- House Fenwick
- House Graves of Eaglegrave
- House Haye

These are landed knightly families holding estates in the Grassy Vale country. House Dunmere has a substantial manor and mill-weir, while the Fenwicks and Hayes hold more modest estates.
