The Durrandons ruled the Stormlands before the Conquest.

## History

Traditionally founded by Durran Godsgrief, the dynasty ended its independent rule when [King Argilac Durrandon](/characters/argilac-durrandon/) died in the Last Storm.

## Family

His daughter [Lady Argella Durrandon](/characters/argella-durrandon/) married Orys Baratheon; their descendants preserved the Storm King bloodline.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree houseId="house:durrandon" />

## Lordship and household

The former royal seat is [Storm's End](/places/storm-s-end/). House Baratheon adopted the Durrandon arms and words.
