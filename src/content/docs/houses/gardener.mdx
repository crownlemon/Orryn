The Gardeners ruled the Reach from Highgarden before the Conquest.

## History

The dynasty traced its origins to the legendary Garth the Gardener. After a succession crisis, Mern VI restored the royal seat with the aid of the Tyrell stewards. The last king, [King Mern IX Gardener](/characters/mern-ix-gardener/), fell at the Field of Fire.

## Family

[King Mern VI Gardener](/characters/mern-vi-gardener/)'s youngest daughter married [Ser Robert Tyrell](/characters/robert-tyrell-steward/), establishing the Tyrell claim to Gardener descent. No continuous pedigree is invented.

## Lordship and household

[Highgarden](/places/highgarden/) passed to the Tyrells at the Conquest. There is no reigning Gardener in 208 AC.
