**House Tarly** holds [Horn Hill](/places/horn-hill/) in [The Reach](/realm/the-reach/). Its immediate overlord is [House Tyrell](/houses/tyrell/).

## Family

[Ser Samwyle Tarly](/characters/samwyle-tarly/) is the eldest son and heir. His unnamed younger brother is father of [Ser Denys Tarly](/characters/denys-tarly/).

Recorded head: [Lord Roderick Tarly](/characters/roderick-tarly/).
