**House Arryn** is the ruling dynasty of [The Vale of Arryn](/realm/vale/) in the historical baseline.

## History

The Arryn dynasty retained authority in the Vale after the Conquest and received the wardenship of the East.

## Family

The 208 AC ruling family has not yet been reconstructed from dated evidence.
