**House Peake** holds [Starpike](/places/starpike/) in [The Reach](/realm/the-reach/). Its immediate overlord in the 208 AC campaign register is [House Tyrell](/houses/tyrell/).

## History

Before 196 AC the Peakes held Starpike, Dunstonbury and Whitegrove. Lord Unwin served as Hand during Aegon III's minority. Gormon's rebellion led to forfeiture of two castles. Any subsequent redistribution follows the campaign register, not later published events.

*Historical reference:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Peake](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Peake).
