**House Greyjoy** is the ruling dynasty of [The Iron Islands](/realm/iron-islands/) in the historical baseline.

## History

After Aegon's campaign against the islands, their nobles chose the Greyjoys as their ruling house.

## Family

The 208 AC ruling family has not yet been reconstructed from dated evidence.
