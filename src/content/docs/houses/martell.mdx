**House Nymeros Martell** is the ruling dynasty associated with Sunspear.

## History

The Martells maintained Dornish independence through the Conquest. Maron's marriage to Daenerys accompanied the negotiated union of 187 AC.

## Family

[Prince Maron Martell](/characters/maron-martell/), [Queen Myriah Martell](/characters/myriah-martell/).
