**House Nymeros Martell** is the ruling dynasty of [Dorne](/realm/dorne/) in the historical baseline.

## History

Dorne retained its independence during Aegon's Conquest and joined the realm under Daeron II through a peaceful settlement.

## Family

The 208 AC ruling family has not yet been reconstructed from dated evidence.
