**House Redwyne of the Arbor** is one of the great noble houses of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Its island lands and maritime resources give it importance beyond its formal standing within the lordship of the Reach.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="house:redwyne" />

## The Arbor and royal service

In the reign of King Daeron II, **Lord Theomar Redwyne** rules the Arbor and holds the royal office of Master of Ships. His position at court and his family's commercial interests provide political influence independent of the influence of his daughter at Highgarden.

Relations with House Rowan of Goldengrove have long been marked by rivalry over influence and standing, but neither family is invariably opposed to the other. The Redwynes also possess connections with the coastal Blackbars of Bandallon.

## The family of Lord Theomar

Lord Theomar's daughter **Lady Alys** is married to Ser Garth Tyrell, heir to Highgarden. Their eldest son is Ser Harlen Tyrell. His son **Ser Arron Redwyne**, Lady Alys's younger brother, serves as Lord Seneschal of Highgarden by Lord Leo's appointment. Arron's son Hugh is a young member of the Highgarden court and a first cousin of Harlen.

Lord Theomar's younger sister, Lady Melara, married a former Lord Blackbar. Their son Lord Arstan Blackbar rules Bandallon. These marriages make House Redwyne influential at Highgarden and among the maritime nobility without making every relation its subordinate.
