House Caswell of Bitterbridge holds the Roseroad crossing of the Mander.

## Family

Lord Armond's first marriage produced Lady Jeyne Meadows. His second wife, Lady Brenna Tyrell, is the mother of Joffrey, heir to Bitterbridge. Ser Cleyton is Armond's nephew.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree houseId="house:caswell" />

## Lordship and household

The lord holds the dignity Defender of the Fords. House Caswell is sworn to Highgarden; its crossing is a strategically important point on the Roseroad.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 2097–2112 and 3537; Worldbook v10.*
