## Overview

House Rowan of Goldengrove is an ancient noble family of the Reach. Its established history and heraldry form part of the background inherited from *A Song of Ice and Fire*.

In the Orryn game, Lord Rycherd Rowan (53) is a proud and effective negotiator who guards his family's political standing.
