**House Rowan of Goldengrove** is an ancient noble house of the northern Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Its arms are a golden tree upon silver. By the time of the Orryn game in 208 AC, Lord Rycherd Rowan is its head and hereditary **Marshal of the Northmarch**, a dignity distinct from the appointed officers of Highgarden.

## History

The Rowans are counted among the ancient families of the Reach. The house's golden-tree emblem is associated with the legendary Rowan Gold-Tree, one of the figures in the traditions surrounding Garth Greenhand. Its family history reaches back into the age of the Gardener kings and the centuries of Targaryen rule.

In the historical settlement established for the Orryn game, the **Marshalship of the Northmarch** passed from House Osgrey to House Rowan in the aftermath of the Dance of the Dragons, approximately 135–145 AC. The precise occasion, instrument and intermediate succession of that grant have not been settled in the campaign records. The title is hereditary in Lord Rycherd's time, rather than an office he holds at the pleasure of Ser Moryn Tyrell.

By the beginning of the present game, Goldengrove has substantial independent authority in the northern Reach. It maintains close relationships with the Hightowers of Oldtown and the Penroses of Parchments through marriage, while an established rivalry with House Redwyne continues to shape its political interests. The Rowans have ties to Ser Moryn Tyrell, who cultivates the old landed nobility, but neither the house nor its hereditary dignity is subordinate to his personal circle.

## Events in 208 AC

The dynastic interests of Goldengrove have acquired new importance during the year. Lord Rycherd's son and heir, **Ser Triston Rowan**, became betrothed to **Lady Joy Penrose** by sealed letters shortly before Chapter 60. Joy is the daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen. This prospective marriage strengthens a connection already present through Lady Clarice Hightower, Lord Rycherd's wife, whose younger sister Lady Alysanne married into the Penroses. The wedding date is not yet established.

The young Lord Harys Oakheart is held at Goldengrove in Lord Rowan's keeping, as recorded in Chapters 61–62. His wardship has become politically significant in connection with the proposed marriage to Lady Elyce Meadows. No such marriage has been concluded.

A separate grievance arose from the dispute at **[Cress Hill](/events/cress-hill/)**, where Highgarden's Justiciar heard a quarrel involving two Rowan vassals and Ser Tom Costen drew a knife. The question of Costen's punishment has become entangled with the lord of Goldengrove's claim to jurisdiction over his own sworn men. Rycherd's position reflects the larger political struggle over customary rights and Highgarden's authority.

## Sworn houses

The following are recorded as **direct vassals of House Rowan** in the Orryn Worldbook v10.

**Minor noble houses**

- House Shermer of Smithyton
- House Stackhouse of Hayrick Hall
- House Varner of Wheatbridge
- House Webber of Coldmoat

**Landed knightly houses**

- House Conklyn of Alderbank
- House Durwell of Durr's Well
- House Osgrey of Standfast

This is a list of currently identified sworn houses, not a claim that no other vassals exist.
