**House Rowan of [Goldengrove](/places/goldengrove/)** is an ancient and powerful noble house of the northern Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. The Rowans bear a golden tree upon silver. Their lord is **[Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/)**, the hereditary Marshal of the Northmarch.

## History

The Rowans count themselves among the ancient lineages of the Reach. Their golden tree recalls Rowan Gold-Tree, the daughter of Garth Greenhand from whom the house claims descent. The family's history stretches back through the rule of the Gardener kings to the centuries of Targaryen dominion.

At Aegon's Conquest, Goldengrove was among the gathering places of the vast host assembled by King Mern IX Gardener and King Loren I Lannister against Aegon the Conqueror. In the reign of Maegor I, Lord Rickard Rowan and Lord Torgen Oakheart defended the followers of Septon Moon beneath the walls of Oldtown. During the Dance of the Dragons, Lord Thaddeus Rowan supported the blacks, fought at the Honeywine and later served as both regent and Hand of the King under Aegon III.

In the settlement that followed the Dance of the Dragons, the **Marshalship of the Northmarch** passed from the Osgreys to the Rowans, sometime around 135–145 AC. It became a hereditary dignity of Goldengrove. The northern marshalship strengthened Rowan authority over a region where many lesser houses held their lands by ancient custom and allegiance.

[Goldengrove](/places/goldengrove/) remains a centre of influence in the northern Reach. Its lords maintain close ties to Oldtown and Parchments through marriage, while their rivalry with House Redwyne touches questions of precedence, patronage and the government of the Reach. Lord Rycherd is associated with Ser Moryn Tyrell, whose influence rests in part upon the support of older landed families, but the Rowans pursue their own political interests.

## Events in 208 AC

The year brought a notable marriage agreement for the Rowan family. Ser Triston Rowan, heir to Goldengrove, was betrothed to Lady Joy Penrose, daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen. The agreement strengthened ties already present through Triston's mother, Lady Clarice Hightower, whose sister Lady Alysanne had married Ser Roland Penrose. Triston and Joy remained betrothed, not yet wed.

The young Lord Harys Oakheart was kept at Goldengrove under Lord Rycherd's guardianship. His position drew the house into discussion of a possible marriage with Lady Elyce Meadows. Rowan also took Ser Alyn Merryweather into his household as a knight.

A dispute at **[Cress Hill](/events/cress-hill/)** raised questions of jurisdiction between Goldengrove and Highgarden. After the Lord Justiciar heard a quarrel between two Rowan vassals, Ser Tom Costen drew a knife in the hall. Lord Rycherd sought to have the knight returned to face judgment before the men whose quarrel had brought the case about.
