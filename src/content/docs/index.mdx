## Westeros in 208 AC

The Seven Kingdoms live beneath the rule of King Daeron II Targaryen, with the memory of the Blackfyre Rebellion still fresh among their lords. Old enmities survive in marriage alliances, wards and inherited offices, even where open war has ended.

In the Reach, Lord Leo Tyrell rules from Highgarden while the ambitions of his sons draw different circles of lords into the affairs of his court. Beyond Highgarden's walls, the ancient houses guard their rights, lands and obligations.
