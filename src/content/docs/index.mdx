## A living alternative history

Established ASOIAF history provides the background up to the Orryn timeline. From the game's beginning onward, **approved Orryn chapters and worldbook records take precedence**. Plans, rumours and beliefs remain distinct from confirmed events.

This is an **initial prototype**. The character and house entries are partial drafts based on known Orryn notes, not a verified conversion of the entire worldbook. For the migration policy, see [Canon and sources](/about/canon-and-sources/).
