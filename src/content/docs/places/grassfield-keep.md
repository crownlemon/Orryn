**Grassfield Keep** is the seat of **[House Meadows](/houses/meadows/)** of Grassy Vale, a prosperous lordly castle in the eastern Reach near the Stormlands border. It stands upon a gentle rise above the town of **[Grassy Vale](/places/grassy-vale/)**, with the Blueburn winding through the meadowlands below and orchards climbing the surrounding slopes.

## Castle

The castle is low, broad and built of grey stone, with a round tower at one corner and a curtain wall repaired in different kinds of stone. A gatehouse, towers, inner gates and a shallow water defence protect the lord's residence. It is a strong country keep rather than a great fortress of the Dornish Marches.

The **inner bailey** is arranged around an old square keep and contains the great hall, lord's solar, family and guest chambers, kitchens, stores, sept, maester's rooms and herb gardens. Grassfield's hall is comparatively light and comfortable, with painted beams, carved floral ornament, woven hangings, broad windows and doors opening towards gardens and private chambers. It is used for household meals, local courts, celebrations and visiting noble families.

The **outer bailey** is busy with the work of the estate. It contains stables, a falcon mews, smithy, barns, granaries, workshops, kitchen gardens, servants' quarters and practice spaces. The squires' lodging has pallets for the boys serving the household's knights, and a scarred table used for their work and lessons. Lord Alaric's upper counting room above the gatehouse contains ledgers and a writing desk.

## Household

**[Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/)** rules the castle with his wife **[Lady Jeyne Caswell](/characters/jeyne-meadows/)**. Their children are Leonette, Matthos and Elyce. During 208 AC, Leonette married Ser Garlan Footly and went to Tumbleton, and Matthos entered the household of Ser Moryn Tyrell at Highgarden.

The steward **Lyman Verrall** oversees the day-to-day business of the estate. Ser Everard Blythe serves as master-at-arms, Ser Wyman Latham as master of horse and hawks, and Maester Perrin advises the family. The household knights include Ser Bertram Corliss, Ser Tobin Whitlow and Ser Lucan Marwick. **[Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/)**, a younger kinsman from the Stormlands, is Lord Alaric's squire.

## Events in 208 AC

Grassfield Keep hosted the marriage of **[Lady Leonette Meadows](/characters/leonette-meadows/)** to Ser Garlan Footly. Among the guests were lords and knights of the Reach and Stormlands, including members of Houses Rowan, Caswell, Merryweather and Ashford. At the feast, Lady Clarice Rowan publicly announced the betrothal of her son Ser Triston to Lady Joy Penrose.

The dry summer strained fodder stocks, rivers and wells across the surrounding lands. Grassfield's horses and grain stores became matters of daily concern, especially while Ser Olyvar Tyrell and other knights debated a response to raids on the Dornish border. Lord Alaric eventually travelled from the keep to Goldengrove in pursuit of discussions about the proposed marriage of his daughter Elyce to Lord Harys Oakheart.
