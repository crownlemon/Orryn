Longtable is the seat of House Merryweather.

## Description

Its position at the confluence of the Mander and Blueburn places the holding in the river country of the Reach.

## Lordship

The lordship is sworn to Highgarden, with Ser Lymond as heir.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 2312–2316; Worldbook v10.*
