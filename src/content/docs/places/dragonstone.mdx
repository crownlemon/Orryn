**Dragonstone** is a royal island castle in Blackwater Bay.

## History

The Targaryens settled here before the fall of Valyria. It was the seat from which Aegon began his conquest.

## Lordship

The title [Prince of Dragonstone](/titles/prince-of-dragonstone/) is held by Prince Baelor in the current snapshot.
