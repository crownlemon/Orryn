**Alder Mill** is recorded in the 208 AC campaign setting.

## Description

Mill complex near Grassy Vale, transferred from Graves to Haye after the rebellion. Fallow holds a twenty-five-year rent-free operating lease; repair costs are recovered through tolls before the proceeds are split equally with Haye. Responsibility for Eaglegrave's historical exemption is disputed.

*Campaign source: Worldbook v10, paragraph 1639.*
