**Merewatch** is a holding recorded in [The Stormlands](/realm/the-stormlands/) in the 208 AC campaign register.

## Description

The lordship lies inland in southern Cape Wrath, ten to twenty miles from the coast, around [The Mere](/places/the-mere/). Its castle controls the outlet of [Greywash](/places/greywash/) and the road bridge. Old Mere is on the west bank and Newbridge on the east. Established settlements include [Brookfield](/places/brookfield/), [Coldharbour](/places/coldharbour/) and [Towerfield](/places/towerfield/). The economy rests on woodland, pasture, mills, fisheries and tolls rather than marsh agriculture. House Mereweather holds beneath House Mertyns.

*Worldbook v10, paragraphs 772–828 and 934–971.*

Geographic anchor: Rainwood, in Mistwood district. The register places the holding in grid area S.

## Lordship

Recorded ruling house or seat holder: [House Mereweather](/houses/mereweather/).
