**Summerhall** is a holding recorded in [The Stormlands](/realm/the-stormlands/) in the 208 AC campaign register.

## Description

The pleasure castle was built by Daeron II and is held by Prince Maekar. It stands two to three days' ride southeast of Grandview, between Grandison and Cafferen country and Storm's End.

*Worldbook v10, paragraphs 1111–1116.*

Geographic anchor: Marcher foothills near Reach border. The register places the holding in grid area W.

## Lordship

Recorded ruling house or seat holder: [House Targaryen](/houses/targaryen/).
