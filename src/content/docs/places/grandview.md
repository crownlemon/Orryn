**Grandview** is the ancestral seat of **[House Grandison](/houses/grandison/)** in the western Stormlands. The castle occupies the end of a long rocky ridge above wooded valleys near the border with the Reach. The land approaches from the east, while the ground falls away steeply on the other sides, affording the castle wide views of the roads and lower country.

## Castle

Grandview is an old, substantial but unostentatious fortress of grey-brown local stone. Its eastern approach crosses a dry ditch before reaching a gatehouse. Stone curtain walls, three towers and the steep slopes of the ridge form its defences. The western wall follows the edge of the height, so that parts of the castle appear to rise directly from the hillside. On clear days the Reach can be seen from the walls.

The oldest part of Grandview is a broad, square keep called the **Old Lion**. Its thick walls shelter the lord's solar, family chambers, archives, treasury, armoury and storerooms, reached by narrow stairs and dark passages. The **inner ward** contains the great hall, kitchens, family and guest apartments, sept, maester's rooms, well and administrative rooms. The hall, with its raised dais, long tables, hunting trophies, old shields and great fireplaces, is the centre of the castle's life. The **outer ward** contains the stables, barracks, smithy, kennels, granaries, workshops and practice yard.

Grandview is better suited to the protection of its lands, local justice and meetings of neighbouring houses than to the grandeur of a royal court.

## Village and lands

Below the eastern gate lies **Grandview village**, a castle settlement built around the widening ridge road and its market green. The village has an inn, a sept, smithy, mill, bakers, craftsmen and the houses of tenants, retainers and castle servants.

The surrounding lordship consists of upland pasture, oak woodland, small valleys, streams, cattle and sheep country, and fields worked for oats and barley. Its economy depends upon wool, horses, livestock, timber, stone and trade with the more prosperous Reach markets. House Gower holds Clovercross in the lower valleys, while the Wagstaffs of Greenward and Elmridges of Elmbridge guard smaller estates and approaches to the castle.

## History

**Lord Lorent Grandison**, an earlier member of the ruling house, was appointed among the regents of the young King Aegon III in 136 AC.

In **196 AC**, during the First Blackfyre Rebellion, the late **[Lord Gawen Grandison](/characters/gawen-grandison/)** delayed his muster at Grandview for nine days after the summons from Storm's End. His mounted men eventually rode on the tenth day, when the Battle of the Redgrass Field had already been won. This delay followed the house long after his death.

Grandview is now ruled by **[Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/)** and his wife Lady Lynesse Hertson. Their daughter Elenei's marriage to Ser Addam Penrose brought the Penrose family to the castle, while their nephew Orryn later went to Grassy Vale to serve **[Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/)**.
