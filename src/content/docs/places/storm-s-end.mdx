**Storm’s End** is a holding recorded in [The Stormlands](/realm/the-stormlands/) in the 208 AC campaign register.

## Description

Storm's End occupies Durran's Point overlooking Shipbreaker Bay. Its enormous curved curtain wall is about one hundred feet high and at least forty feet thick, thicker on the seaward side. The circular Drum Keep contains the chief household and administrative rooms. The castle yard, godswood and protected sea passage are distinct; there is no ordinary safe harbour.

*Worldbook v10, paragraphs 539–570.*

Geographic anchor: Headland above Shipbreaker Bay. The register places the holding in grid area NE.

## Lordship

Recorded ruling house or seat holder: [House Baratheon](/houses/baratheon/), [Lord of Storm's End](/titles/lord-of-storms-end/). Recorded head: [Lord Edric Baratheon](/characters/edric-baratheon/).
