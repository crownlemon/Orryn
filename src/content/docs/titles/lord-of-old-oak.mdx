**Lord of Old Oak** is a hereditary lordship associated with House Oakheart and its seat, Old Oak.

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is Lord Harys Oakheart. The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 3554.*
