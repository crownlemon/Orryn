**Lord of Parchments** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Penrose](/houses/penrose/) and its seat, Parchments.

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 1149.*
