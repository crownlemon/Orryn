The **Lord Commander of the Kingsguard** leads the royal sworn brotherhood and participates in the king's council.

## Nature of the title

The king appoints a commander from among the sworn brothers. The office is not inherited by the knight's family.

## Holders

**In 207–208 AC:** [Ser Willem Wylde](/characters/willem-wylde/), as established by Worldbook v10.

[Ser Robin Massey](/characters/robin-massey/) received an appointment in 133 AC which was set aside. He is recorded separately from the established succession. Unplaced names are omitted rather than assuming that they served before 207 AC.

## History

The order was established in 10 AC. The first commander, Ser Corlys Velaryon, is distinct from his later namesake, the Sea Snake. During the Dance, Rhaenyra maintained a separate [Queensguard](/titles/lord-commander-queensguard/).
