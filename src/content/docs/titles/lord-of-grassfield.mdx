**Lord of Grassfield Keep** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Meadows](/houses/meadows/) and its seat, [Grassfield Keep](/places/grassfield-keep/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Worldbook v10, current title register.*
