**Lord of the Arbor** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Redwyne](/houses/redwyne/) and its seat, the Arbor.

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Theomar Redwyne](/characters/theomar-redwyne/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 2845.*
