**Lord of Coldmoat** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Webber](/houses/webber/) and its seat, [Coldmoat](/places/coldmoat/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Wyman Webber](/characters/wyman-webber/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 3647.*
