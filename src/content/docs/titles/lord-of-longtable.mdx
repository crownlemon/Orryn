**Lord of Longtable** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Merryweather](/houses/merryweather/) and its seat, [Longtable](/places/longtable/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is Lord Merryweather (given name unrecorded). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 2326.*
