**Marshal of the Dornish Marches** is an appointment in the Reach in the current Orryn continuity.

## Nature of the title

The record identifies the title separately from its holder. No permanent hereditary attachment to a noble house is established in the knowledge model.

## Holders

The recorded holder is Lord Roderick Tarly. The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.
