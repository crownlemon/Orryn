**Lord of Bitterbridge** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Caswell](/houses/caswell/) and its seat, [Bitterbridge](/places/bitterbridge/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Armond Caswell](/characters/armond-caswell/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 2112.*
