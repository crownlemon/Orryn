**Lord of Grandview** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Grandison](/houses/grandison/) and its seat, [Grandview](/places/grandview/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Worldbook v10, current title register.*
