**High Marshal of the Reach** is a recorded dignity in the current Orryn continuity.

## Nature of the title

The record identifies the title separately from its holder. No permanent hereditary attachment to a noble house is established in the knowledge model.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Leo Tyrell](/characters/leo-tyrell/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.
