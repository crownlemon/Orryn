**Lord of Storm's End** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Baratheon](/houses/baratheon/) and its seat, Storm’s End.

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Edric Baratheon](/characters/edric-baratheon/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 895.*
