The **Hand of the King** is the crown's senior appointed councillor and may exercise delegated royal authority.

## Nature of the title

The office is personal rather than hereditary. Its responsibilities encompass government, justice and the direction of the council.

## Holders

**In 207–208 AC:** [Prince Baelor Targaryen](/characters/baelor-breakspear/), as established by Worldbook v10.

The published record also names an unnamed Lord Bracken, an unnamed Lord Butterwell and Lord Jon Hightower under Aegon IV. Their precise tenures are not established here; the latter is not assumed to be identical to the campaign's Lord Jon Hightower. The Hand under Viserys II is not identified. Lord Corlys Velaryon served the rival claimant Rhaenyra, not Aegon II.

## History

The institution dates to the Conquest. The Dance divided royal authority, while the minority of Aegon III brought frequent changes of administration. The defeat of the Blackfyre rebellion forms the background to the present administration. Periods have year precision only; the list is not evidence for an exact day of transition.
