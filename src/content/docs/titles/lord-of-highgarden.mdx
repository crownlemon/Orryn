**Lord of Highgarden** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Tyrell](/houses/tyrell/) and its seat, Highgarden.

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Leo Tyrell](/characters/leo-tyrell/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 2509.*
