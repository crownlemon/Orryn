This command belonged to the royal guard of Rhaenyra Targaryen during the contested succession of the Dance.

## Nature of the title

An appointment to lead the claimant's sworn protectors, distinct from the command recognized by Aegon II.

## History

The competing royal guards reflected the division of allegiance during the war. This is a historical office; no incumbent is established in the current campaign.
