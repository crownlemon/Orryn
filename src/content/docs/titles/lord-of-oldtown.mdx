**Lord of Oldtown** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Hightower](/houses/hightower/) and its seat, [Oldtown](/places/oldtown/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Jon Hightower](/characters/jon-hightower/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 3085.*
