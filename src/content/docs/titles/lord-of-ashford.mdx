**Lord of Ashford** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Ashford](/houses/ashford/) and its seat, [Ashford](/places/ashford/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is Lord Ashford (given name unrecorded). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Householdbook v12, reference 2209.*
