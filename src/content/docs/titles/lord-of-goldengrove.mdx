**Lord of Goldengrove** is a hereditary lordship associated with [House Rowan](/houses/rowan/) and its seat, [Goldengrove](/places/goldengrove/).

## Nature of the title

The lordship belongs to the recorded house and is distinct from the person holding it.

## Holders

The recorded holder is [Lord Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/). The current register does not establish a complete historical succession.

*Source: Worldbook v10, current title register.*
