**The Riverlands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### Successive river kings and the Dance

[House Justman](/houses/justman/) and [House Teague](/houses/teague/) were earlier royal houses. [King Humfrey I Teague](/characters/humfrey-i-teague/) fell in religious conflict; [King Harwyn Hoare](/characters/harwyn-hoare/) of [House Hoare](/houses/hoare/) defeated [King Arrec Durrandon](/characters/arrec-durrandon/). Harrenhal became the seat of [King Harren Hoare](/characters/harren-hoare/). During the Dance [Battle of the Burning Mill](/events/battle-burning-mill/) reflected the old Blackwood–Bracken rivalry, while [House Frey](/houses/frey/) supported Rhaenyra.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Justman](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Justman); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Teague](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Teague); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Hoare](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Hoare); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Frey](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Frey); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Battle of the Burning Mill](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Battle_of_the_Burning_Mill).

After the fall of House Hoare, Aegon granted the Tullys authority over the riverlands.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Tully](/houses/tully/), associated with [Riverrun](/places/riverrun/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
