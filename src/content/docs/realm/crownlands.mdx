**The Crownlands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

Aegon established the Crownlands around Blackwater Bay as the monarchy's own territory.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Targaryen](/houses/targaryen/), associated with [King's Landing](/places/kings-landing/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
