**The Crownlands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### Old Blackwater lordships

[House Darklyn](/houses/darklyn/) and [House Mooton](/houses/mooton/) opposed Aegon at [Aegon's first test](/events/aegons-first-test/). [Duskendale](/places/duskendale/) remained a major port during the first two centuries of Targaryen rule. Later outcomes are excluded.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Darklyn](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Darklyn); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Duskendale](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Duskendale); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Mooton](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Mooton); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Conquest](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Conquest).

Aegon established the Crownlands around Blackwater Bay as the monarchy's own territory.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Targaryen](/houses/targaryen/), associated with [King's Landing](/places/kings-landing/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
