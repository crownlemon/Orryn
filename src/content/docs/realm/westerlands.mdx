**The Westerlands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### Royal succession and the Dance

The transition from [House Casterly](/houses/casterly/) is legendary. The marriage of [King Joffrey Lannister](/characters/joffrey-lydden/) of [House Lydden](/houses/lydden/) to [Unnamed Princess Lannister](/characters/lannister-unnamed-daughter-gerold-iii/) illustrates the accommodation of Andal nobility. [Lord Humfrey Lefford](/characters/humfrey-lefford/) and [Ser Adrian Tarbeck](/characters/adrian-tarbeck/) served during the Dance; [Lady Johanna Lannister](/characters/johanna-lannister/) resisted the ironborn.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Lannister](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Lannister); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Humfrey Lefford](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Humfrey_Lefford); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Dalton Greyjoy](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Dalton_Greyjoy).

The Lannisters had ruled the region as kings before becoming vassals of the Iron Throne.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Lannister](/houses/lannister/), associated with [Casterly Rock](/places/casterly-rock/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
