**The Vale of Arryn** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### The Bronze Kings and Andal conquest

[House Royce](/houses/royce/) once ruled as Bronze Kings. [King Yorwyck VI Royce](/characters/yorwyck-vi-royce/) and [King Robar II Royce](/characters/robar-ii-royce/) belong to that era. [Battle of the Seven Stars](/events/battle-seven-stars/) established the supremacy of [King Artys I Arryn](/characters/artys-i-arryn/). A later Royce marriage connected the succession of [Lord Hubert Arryn](/characters/hubert-arryn/) to the old dynasty.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Royce](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Royce); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Arryn](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Arryn); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Battle of the Seven Stars](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Battle_of_the_Seven_Stars).

The Arryn dynasty retained authority in the Vale after the Conquest and received the wardenship of the East.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Arryn](/houses/arryn/), associated with [The Eyrie](/places/eyrie/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
