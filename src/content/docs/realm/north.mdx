**The North** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### Kings of Winter and Red Kings

Winterfell absorbed other northern kingdoms. [King Rogar Bolton](/characters/rogar-bolton-huntsman/) and [King Royce II Bolton](/characters/royce-ii-bolton/) belong to the old Red Kingship. [King Theon Stark](/characters/theon-stark-hungry-wolf/) opposed the Andals at [Battle of the Weeping Water](/events/battle-weeping-water/); [King Rodrik Stark](/characters/rodrik-stark-ancient/) is associated with the recovery of Bear Island. Ancient dates remain uncertain.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Stark](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Stark); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Bolton](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Bolton); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Andal invasion](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Andal_invasion).

The Stark dynasty surrendered its former kingship to Aegon I and retained regional authority under the new monarchy.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Stark](/houses/stark/), associated with [Winterfell](/places/winterfell/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
