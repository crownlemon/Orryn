**The North** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

The Stark dynasty surrendered its former kingship to Aegon I and retained regional authority under the new monarchy.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Stark](/houses/stark/), associated with [Winterfell](/places/winterfell/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
