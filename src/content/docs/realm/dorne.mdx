**Dorne** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### Rhoynish migration and unification

[Princess Nymeria](/characters/nymeria-princess-rhoynar/) and [Prince Mors Martell](/characters/mors-martell/) founded the Nymeros Martell dynasty. In [Nymeria's War](/events/nymerias-war/) their last great opponent was [King Yorick V Yronwood](/characters/yorick-v-yronwood/). These old alliances explain historical rivalries, not current factions.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Nymeria](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Nymeria); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Martell](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Martell); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Nymeria's War](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Nymeria%27s_War); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Yorick V Yronwood](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Yorick_V_Yronwood).

Dorne retained its independence during Aegon's Conquest and joined the realm under Daeron II through a peaceful settlement.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Nymeros Martell](/houses/martell/), associated with [Sunspear](/places/sunspear/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
