**Dorne** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

Dorne retained its independence during Aegon's Conquest and joined the realm under Daeron II through a peaceful settlement.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Nymeros Martell](/houses/martell/), associated with [Sunspear](/places/sunspear/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
