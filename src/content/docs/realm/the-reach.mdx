**The Reach** is one of the largest and most fertile regions of the Seven Kingdoms. Its lords owe allegiance to **House Tyrell of Highgarden**, whose power rests upon the lands of the Mander and the numerous ancient families sworn to its rule.

Oldtown, the Arbor, the Shield Islands and the northern approaches around [Goldengrove](/places/goldengrove/) are among the region's centres of wealth and influence. Houses Hightower and Redwyne command resources and relationships beyond their immediate estates, while houses such as Rowan, Tarly, Caswell, Ashford and Peake preserve their own histories and privileges.

## History and government

### The ancient vassal houses

[Lord Rickard Rowan](/characters/rickard-rowan-faith/) and [Lord Torgen Oakheart](/characters/torgen-oakheart/) supported [Septon Moon](/characters/septon-moon/). The Florents claim close Gardener descent through the legendary Florys; the Tarlys trace their origins to legendary brothers. [Lord Manfryd Redwyne](/characters/manfryd-redwyne-historical/) was father to Ser Ryam. The expulsion of the Manderlys is associated with [Lord Lorimar Peake](/characters/lorimar-peake/) and [King Perceon III Gardener](/characters/perceon-iii-gardener/). No complete pedigree to the present families is assumed.

### The Gardener kings and the Conquest

[House Gardener](/houses/gardener/) ruled the Reach before the [Field of Fire](/events/field-of-fire/). The Tyrells succeeded at Highgarden. The Dance divided the region at the [Honeywine](/events/battle-honeywine/) and [Tumbleton](/events/first-tumbleton/).

### Highgarden in 208 AC

Lord Leo Tyrell rules the Reach in the reign of Daeron II. Ser Garth, his heir, favours closer administration of obligations and revenues. Ser Moryn has the friendship of many older landed houses, among them the Rowans of Goldengrove. Ser Olyvar's interests lie principally among the knights and marcher and maritime lords. These circles overlap and their members retain loyalties and ambitions of their own.

The years following the First Blackfyre Rebellion have left the region with uneasy reconciliations, old grievances and new marriages between families once divided by war.
