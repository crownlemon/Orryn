**The Iron Islands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

After Aegon's campaign against the islands, their nobles chose the Greyjoys as their ruling house.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Greyjoy](/houses/greyjoy/), associated with [Pyke](/places/pyke/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
