**The Iron Islands** is one of the regions of Westeros.

## History and government

### The old kings and the Red Kraken

[House Greyiron](/houses/greyiron/) made kingship hereditary under [King Urras Greyiron](/characters/urras-greyiron-redhand/). House Hoare extended rule across the Riverlands. During the Dance [Lord Dalton Greyjoy](/characters/dalton-greyjoy/) attacked the western coast; [Lord Toron Greyjoy](/characters/toron-greyjoy/) succeeded him amid internal conflict.

*References:* [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Greyiron](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Greyiron); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: King of the Iron Islands](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/King_of_the_Iron_Islands); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Hoare](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Hoare); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: House Greyjoy](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_Greyjoy); [A Wiki of Ice and Fire: Dalton Greyjoy](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Dalton_Greyjoy).

After Aegon's campaign against the islands, their nobles chose the Greyjoys as their ruling house.

## Houses and places

The principal ruling dynasty is [House Greyjoy](/houses/greyjoy/), associated with [Pyke](/places/pyke/). Other regional houses and local history still require dated source review.
