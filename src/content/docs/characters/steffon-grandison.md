**Ser Steffon Grandison** is a younger son of [Lord Gawen Grandison](/characters/gawen-grandison/) and brother of the reigning [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/). He maintains a separate household on family lands near Grandview.


## Appearance

Steffon has a sturdy build, dark brown hair, a trimmed beard and grey-blue eyes. His reserved bearing contrasts with his elder brother's genial manner.

## Character

Steffon is practical, careful with figures and often a sound judge of consequences. He is sharply critical of waste and overpromising, and can be ungenerous with praise or sympathy. He believes he would manage Grandview more prudently than Alester, a conviction that both strengthens his advice and deepens his resentment when it is ignored.

## Family

Steffon married [Lady Maris Gower](/characters/maris-grandison/). Their children are [Lady Alyssa Grandison](/characters/alyssa-grandison/) and [Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/). Through his mother Ellyn Meadows, Steffon is first cousin to [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/) of Grassfield Keep, who took Orryn into his service as a squire.

## History

During the First Blackfyre Rebellion, Steffon stood at Grandview while his father delayed answering Storm's End's summons. He counted the men, horses, oats and cost of committing the household to war. He remained an influential voice in the family's financial affairs under Lord Alester and was among the relatives who came to Grandview for Elenei's wedding.
