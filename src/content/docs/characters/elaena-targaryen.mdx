Princess Elaena Targaryen is the wife of [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/) in the Orryn continuity of 208 AC.

## Family

Their children are Robin, Lady Laena, Lady Jocelyn and Lady Joy. Robin is the heir to Parchments; Laena is married to Ser Lyonel Baratheon and Joy is betrothed to Ser Triston Rowan.

## History

Elaena ordinarily resides at the royal court and is recorded among the visiting family members at Parchments. Her marriage supplies a royal connection to Lord Ronnel's branch independent of the Targaryen ancestry of Lady Alysanne Hightower.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1149–1152 and 1167.*
