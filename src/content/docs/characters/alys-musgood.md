**Lady Alys Musgood**, born **Grandison**, is a daughter of [Lord Gawen Grandison](/characters/gawen-grandison/) and [Lady Ellyn Grandison](/characters/ellyn-grandison/), and a sister of the present Lord of Grandview. She married into House Musgood.

## Appearance

Alys has brown hair, lively grey-green eyes and a taste for fashionable gowns and tasteful jewellery.

## Character

Alys is witty, sociable and well informed about the affairs of her relatives. She also meddles freely in other people's concerns, repeating part of a story or arranging an intervention so that she may be needed to resolve the resulting difficulty. Her bluntness can be entertaining to strangers and uncomfortable to those closest to her.

## Family

Her brothers are [Alester](/characters/alester-grandison/), [Steffon](/characters/steffon-grandison/) and [Raymont](/characters/raymont-grandison/). She retains a strong interest in the decisions of Grandview despite her marriage into House Musgood.

## History

Alys returned to Grandview for family gatherings, including the preparations surrounding the marriage of her niece Elenei to Ser Addam Penrose. Her presence in the family household was seldom without an opinion about its affairs.
