**Lady Ceryse Oakheart**, born Tyrell, is the widowed daughter of Lord Leo Tyrell and Lady Arlanna Peake. She governs Old Oak as regent for her young son, **Lord Harys Oakheart**.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="person:ceryse-oakheart" />

## The Oakheart succession

Ceryse married the former Lord Oakheart before the First Blackfyre Rebellion. Her husband supported Daemon Blackfyre and died during the conflict or its aftermath. The family's position after the royal victory was marked by penalties and demands for surety.

Her son Harys holds the title of Lord of Old Oak but has been raised at Goldengrove under Lord Rowan, held for the good conduct of his house. Other children remain in Lady Arlanna's care at Highgarden. Ceryse administers Old Oak in her son's name rather than holding the lordship by right of birth.

## Position and family

Ceryse is closely tied both to the ruling Tyrell house and to the families whose loyalties were contested during the rebellion. Her regency preserves Oakheart continuity amid a difficult settlement, while her son's separation from Old Oak places limits upon the immediate independence of the household.
