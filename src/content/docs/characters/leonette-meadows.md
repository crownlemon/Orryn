**Lady Leonette Footly**, born **Meadows**, is the eldest daughter of [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/) and [Lady Jeyne Meadows](/characters/jeyne-meadows/). She married Ser Garlan Footly, heir to Tumbleton, in 208 AC.

## Appearance

Leonette has chestnut hair, green-brown eyes and a lively smile. She favours bright gowns, ribbons and jewellery and carries herself confidently at gatherings.

## Character

Leonette is bold, charming and eager to be the centre of attention. She notices much of what occurs in noble company, though her delight in passing on confidences can make her indiscreet or hurtful.

## Family

Leonette is the elder sister of [Matthos Meadows](/characters/matthos-meadows/) and [Lady Elyce Meadows](/characters/elyce-meadows/). Through her marriage to Ser Garlan she became connected to House Footly, whose lord Bennard rules Tumbleton.

## History

Leonette's wedding at Grassfield Keep in 208 AC brought many lords and knights of the Reach and Stormlands to her father's hall. Lady Clarice Rowan announced the betrothal of her son Triston to Lady Joy Penrose during the festivities. Leonette left Grassfield for Tumbleton with her husband after the celebrations.
