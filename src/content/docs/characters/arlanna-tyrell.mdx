**Lady Arlanna Tyrell**, born of House Peake, is the wife of Lord Leo Tyrell and Lady of Highgarden. She is the elder sister of Lord Gormon Peake of Starpike.

## Family

Arlanna is mother to Ser Garth, Ser Moryn, Lady Ceryse, Ser Olyvar and Lady Brenna. Their marriages connect Highgarden with the Redwynes of the Arbor, Ashfords, Oakhearts, Hewetts and Caswells. These connections make the Tyrell court a meeting place for many interests without eliminating old rivalries.

## History

### The court of Highgarden

Arlanna governs much of the domestic and ceremonial life at Highgarden, including hospitality, household appointments and the precedence of ladies and guests. Her manners are exacting, and her approval or disapproval carries weight among those seeking access to the ruling family.

She remains devoted to Lord Leo, but retains a strong sense of the antiquity and dignity of her birth house. The penalties suffered by House Peake after the First Blackfyre Rebellion remain a source of grievance.
