Ser Jon Penrose is a knight of House Penrose and a son of Ser Roland Penrose and Lady Alysanne Hightower.

## Family

His siblings are Lady Aelinor Targaryen and Ser Addam Penrose. His uncle is [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/), and his maternal grandparents are Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree personId="person:jon-penrose" />

## History

In 208 AC, Jon is in his early twenties and is recorded as a household knight at Parchments, where his widowed mother is the senior resident member of the dynasty.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1152, 1162 and 1194.*
