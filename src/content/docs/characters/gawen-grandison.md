**Lord Gawen Grandison** was a former Lord of Grandview and the father of the present [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/). He is remembered particularly for his actions during the First Blackfyre Rebellion.

## Appearance

Gawen was broad-shouldered, with greying brown hair, a carefully trimmed beard and blue-grey eyes. An old riding injury left him with a stiff leg.

## Character

Gawen was regarded as patient and even-handed, but he was slow to decide matters that required firmness. His gentleness and willingness to postpone a choice earned him affection while allowing difficult problems to grow. He particularly indulged his eldest son Alester.

## Family

Gawen married [Lady Ellyn Meadows](/characters/ellyn-grandison/). Their children were [Alester](/characters/alester-grandison/), [Steffon](/characters/steffon-grandison/), [Raymont](/characters/raymont-grandison/) and [Alys](/characters/alys-musgood/). Through Ellyn, his descendants are related to [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/) of Grassy Vale.

## History

When Daemon Blackfyre raised his rebellion in 196 AC, Grandview received a call from Storm's End to muster its men. Gawen delayed for nine days while sending messages seeking direction from his overlord; no answer reached him in time. Though his elder son Alester pressed for a prompt march, the Grandison troops did not ride until the tenth day, after the battle at Redgrass Field had already been won. The delay became a lasting subject of criticism, and Grandview's supposed prudence was invoked derisively by its neighbours. Gawen died in his sixties and was succeeded by Alester.
