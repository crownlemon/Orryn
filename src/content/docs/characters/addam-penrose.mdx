Ser Addam Penrose is a knight of House Penrose, the son of Ser Roland Penrose and Lady Alysanne Hightower.

## Family

His siblings are Lady Aelinor Targaryen and Ser Jon Penrose. He is married to [Lady Elenei Grandison](/characters/elenei-grandison/), daughter of Lord Alester Grandison.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree personId="person:addam-penrose" />

## History

Addam's marriage to Elenei took place in 207 AC. In 208 AC he is recorded among the household knights at Parchments. The marriage provides a direct link between [House Penrose](/houses/penrose/) and [House Grandison](/houses/grandison/).

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1152 and 1163; recorded marriage of 207 AC.*
