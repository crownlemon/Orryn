**Lady Roslin Rowan** is the daughter of [Lord Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/) and [Lady Clarice Rowan](/characters/clarice-rowan/) of Goldengrove, and sister to [Ser Triston Rowan](/characters/triston-rowan/), the heir.

## Appearance and character

### Appearance

Roslin has a long neck and a composed face that often reveals little. She has worn her hair beneath a pale veil at noble gatherings, although embarrassment or anger can bring colour sharply to her cheeks.

### Character

Roslin is observant, strong-willed and conscious of the prestige of her Rowan and Hightower blood. She is impatient with being slighted and may answer condescension with cruelty of her own. She understands the value of dynastic marriages, but resents being praised chiefly for her birth or future usefulness.

## Family

Roslin resides with her parents at Goldengrove. Her brother Triston is betrothed to Lady Joy Penrose, a daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen.

## History

Roslin accompanied her parents and brother to the wedding of Lady Leonette Meadows at Grassfield Keep in 208 AC. At the celebrations, the Rowan family announced the betrothal of her brother Triston to Lady Joy Penrose, a daughter of the royal household's Master of Coin.

Joy later arrived at Goldengrove and became a guest of the family whose heir she was to marry. The two young women did not always agree over the privileges of the future lady of the castle. When Joy spoke of the high table as something that would one day belong to Triston, Roslin reminded her that Lord Rycherd still held the seat. Lady Clarice intervened to end the exchange.

Roslin was also among the young women at Goldengrove during Lord Alaric Meadows's visit, keeping company with Joy and Myranda Webber while other members of the household attended to the guests and to the difficulties caused by the dry summer.
