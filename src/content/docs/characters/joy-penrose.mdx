Lady Joy Penrose is a daughter of [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/) and Princess Elaena Targaryen, of [House Penrose](/houses/penrose/). Her betrothal connects Parchments to Goldengrove.

## Family

Her siblings are Robin, Lady Jocelyn and [Lady Laena Baratheon](/characters/laena-baratheon/). Her prospective husband, [Ser Triston Rowan](/characters/triston-rowan/), also has Targaryen ancestry through his mother, Lady Clarice Hightower.

## History

The marriage agreement was established in Chapter 60 of 208 AC. At the current point in the chronicles, the pair remain betrothed, not married.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1151 and 1170; Chapter 60.*
