**Lady Elyce Meadows** is the youngest daughter of [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/) and [Lady Jeyne Meadows](/characters/jeyne-meadows/) of Grassfield Keep.

## Appearance

Elyce has soft brown hair, wide hazel eyes, freckles and a small, youthful frame. Her feelings are often visible in her expression.

## Character

Elyce is affectionate, interested in horses and quick to notice what other people are doing. She can also be jealous, inclined to embellish a story and quick to present herself as the wronged party when an argument goes against her.

## Family

Her older siblings are [Leonette](/characters/leonette-meadows/) and [Matthos](/characters/matthos-meadows/). Elyce remained at Grassfield when Leonette married and Matthos left for Highgarden.

## History

In 208 AC, Lady Arlanna Tyrell proposed a marriage between Elyce and Lord Harys Oakheart, the young ward of Lord Rowan at Goldengrove. Elyce's parents objected to the manner of the proposal. When Lord Alaric set out to meet Rowan, Elyce privately asked Orryn Grandison to learn whether Harys rode well and liked horses. No betrothal was concluded.
