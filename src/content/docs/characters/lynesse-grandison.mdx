**Lady Lynesse Grandison**, born **Hertson**, is the wife of [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/) and lady of Grandview.

## Appearance and character

### Appearance

Lynesse is a soft-spoken woman with brown hair, hazel eyes and modest but elegant clothing. Her manner is pleasant, particularly when receiving guests and arranging household hospitality.

### Character

Lynesse is attentive to the details of a noble household and eager to be agreeable. She can, however, be indecisive, easily persuaded by whoever has most recently spoken to her and hesitant to acknowledge an unpleasant consequence. Her willingness to promise a favour sometimes leaves her household officers with incompatible instructions.

## Family

She is married to Lord Alester and mother to [Arlan](/characters/arlan-grandison/), the heir, and [Elenei](/characters/elenei-grandison/). Through marriage she connects the ruling Grandison family to House Hertson.

## History

Lynesse became Lady of Grandview through her marriage to Lord Alester Grandison. She undertook the responsibilities of hospitality and household management that accompanied a castle frequently visited by relatives and neighbouring families.

The wedding of her daughter Elenei to Ser Addam Penrose brought these duties to the fore. Visitors from Parchments required accommodation in Grandview's guest chambers, including rooms within the Old Lion. Lynesse supervised the linen, rooms and arrangements while seeking to honour the preferences of the visiting Penroses. Her tendency to agree readily with requests sometimes left her household servants and nephew Orryn to reconcile contradictory instructions.

After Elenei's marriage, Lynesse remained at Grandview with Alester and their son Arlan, who was being raised as heir to the castle.
