**Ser Lyonel Baratheon** is the eldest son of Lord Edric Baratheon and heir to [Storm's End](/houses/baratheon/). In 208 AC he is married to Lady Laena, born of House Penrose, and is the father of their young son Ormund.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="person:lyonel-baratheon" />

## Appearance and character

Lyonel is tall and broad-shouldered, with thick black hair, bright blue eyes and a ready smile. His confidence, prowess in arms and loud good humour have made him popular among the knights of Storm's End. His enthusiasms also carry costs for those charged with the castle's affairs.

His brothers are Ser Boremund and Ser Gowen. The household has long known that Lyonel and Gowen treat their quieter brother as a target for jokes, although Boremund is a dependable presence in the administration of the family.

## Family

Lady Laena is a daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen. Their marriage joins the Baratheon heir to a branch of the royal family through Laena's mother. Their son Ormund stands after Lyonel in the expected succession.
