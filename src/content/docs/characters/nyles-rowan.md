**Ser Nyles Rowan** is the younger brother of [Lord Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/) of Goldengrove, and a knight of the northern Reach.


## Character

Nyles is an experienced horseman, proud of Rowan standing and eager to make his own name. His aggressive and boastful temperament makes him impatient with any suggestion that he serves only as his elder brother's representative.

## Family

Nyles is brother to Lord Rycherd and [Lady Mina Webber](/characters/mina-webber/), born Rowan. He is an uncle of Ser Triston Rowan and Lady Roslin Rowan, the children of Lord Rycherd and Lady Clarice.

## History

In 208 AC, Nyles rode south with Ser Olyvar Tyrell and other knights following raids on the Dornish border. No general levy had been called. Rycherd watched his brother depart Goldengrove without forbidding the journey and maintained that Nyles was a grown man able to make his own choices.
