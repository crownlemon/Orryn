Lady Aelinor Targaryen, born Penrose, is the daughter of the late Ser Roland Penrose and Lady Alysanne Hightower.

## Family

Her brothers are Ser Jon and Ser Addam Penrose. Through her mother she is a granddaughter of Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen. Her marriage to Prince Aerys is distinct from Lord Ronnel's marriage to Princess Elaena.

## History

The 208 AC register identifies Aelinor as a visiting relative of Parchments and the wife of Prince Aerys. Later royal succession is outside the current historical snapshot.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1152 and 1172.*
