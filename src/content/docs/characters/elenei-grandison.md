**Lady Elenei Penrose**, born **Grandison**, is the daughter of [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/) and [Lady Lynesse Grandison](/characters/lynesse-grandison/) of Grandview. She married Ser Addam Penrose, a member of House Penrose of Parchments.


## Appearance

Elenei has brown hair, bright hazel eyes and a lively smile.

## Character

Elenei is warm, cheerful and affectionate, though easily swayed by flattery and inclined to act before considering consequences. She enjoys company and admiration, and her thoughtlessness can wound people whose good opinion she sincerely values.

## Family

Her brother [Arlan Grandison](/characters/arlan-grandison/) is heir to Grandview. Elenei's husband Ser Addam is a son of the late Ser Roland Penrose and Lady Alysanne Hightower, linking Elenei's family to the Hightower kin of House Rowan.

## History

Elenei's marriage to Ser Addam Penrose was celebrated at Grandview. The wedding occupied her father's household for many weeks, requiring the preparation of the Old Lion's guest chambers and the accommodation of the visiting Penroses. Her cousin Orryn, who was preparing to leave Grandview for service at Grassfield Keep, assisted with some of the arrangements.

After the wedding Elenei lived with her husband's kin at Parchments. She continued to write to Orryn, reporting on her new surroundings and family. In a letter carried to Goldengrove, she described Ser Addam as kind and commented upon the manners of her mother-in-law, Lady Alysanne Penrose. Through her marriage Elenei formed another link between the Grandisons of the western Stormlands and a family with close ties to House Rowan and the Hightowers.
