**Lord Theomar Redwyne** is the lord of the Arbor, head of [House Redwyne](/houses/redwyne/) and Master of Ships to King Daeron II. His family's maritime strength, commerce and royal service give him considerable influence in the Reach and at court.

## Family

Theomar's daughter Lady Alys is married to Ser Garth Tyrell, heir to Highgarden. His younger son Ser Arron serves as Lord Seneschal of Highgarden and is father to Hugh Redwyne, a young courtier there. Theomar's younger sister Lady Melara married the previous Lord Blackbar of Bandallon and is mother of Lord Arstan Blackbar.

## History

### Lordship and royal service

Theomar is experienced in naval and commercial affairs, a patient negotiator and a demanding patron of his family. The office of Master of Ships places him within royal government independently of the Lord of Highgarden, although the Arbor remains a sworn house of the Reach.

He is mindful of the influence his family has obtained through marriage, appointments and wealth. His alliances do not erase the autonomy of related houses, particularly when their interests differ.
