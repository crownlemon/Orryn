**Lady Ellyn Grandison**, born **Meadows**, was the wife of [Lord Gawen Grandison](/characters/gawen-grandison/) and a former lady of Grandview. She was the sister of the late Lord Meadows and paternal aunt of [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/).

## Appearance and character

### Appearance

Ellyn had chestnut hair and brown-green eyes, and favoured finely made gowns with elegant embroidery and jewellery in the manner of the Reach.

### Character

Ellyn was orderly, exacting and proud of the refinements she brought from her Meadows family. Her household ran according to demanding standards, though she could be harsh with servants and relatives who disappointed her. She was remembered both for bringing discipline to Grandview and for withholding approval when others needed it.

## Family

Her marriage to Lord Gawen connected House Meadows of Grassy Vale and House Grandison of Grandview across the regional border. Their sons were Alester, Steffon and Raymont; their daughter Alys married into House Musgood. Through Steffon, Ellyn was grandmother to Lady Alyssa and Orryn Grandison.

## History

Ellyn married Lord Gawen Grandison and brought the habits and expectations of her Meadows family to the halls of Grandview. She took a particular interest in the ordering of guest chambers, the conduct of servants and the appearance of a lordly household. Her standards were remembered long after her death by those who had served under her, though not always with affection.

Her descendants retained close ties to Grassy Vale. Her son Steffon's youngest boy, Orryn, was sent to become a squire under Ellyn's nephew Lord Alaric Meadows. Orryn continued to remember his grandmother's rules when faced with the disorder of Grandview's guest chambers before Elenei's wedding, particularly her insistence that no room should stand with its beds unaired.
