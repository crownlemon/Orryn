Ser Wendell Webber is a household knight at Goldengrove.

## Family

He is married to Lady Mina, sister of Lord Rycherd Rowan. His recorded children include Bryce and Myranda; an elder son is mentioned but not named.

## History

He met Lord Alaric's party below Goldengrove and hosted them on the approach to the castle.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 3644–3660 and 3754; Chapter 66.*
