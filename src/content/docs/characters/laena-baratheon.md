**Lady Laena Baratheon**, born of [House Penrose](/houses/penrose/), is the wife of Ser Lyonel Baratheon, heir to Storm's End. She is the daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen, and the mother of Ormund Baratheon.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="person:laena-baratheon" />

## Appearance and character

Laena is a young noblewoman with dark auburn hair, pale skin and thoughtful grey eyes. She is well educated, and is respected for her ability with correspondence and financial matters. She is regarded as refined and capable in the household, although her manner toward servants is often distant.

## Family and position

Through her father she belongs to the principal Penrose family of Parchments. Through her mother she is a granddaughter of the Targaryen line. Her marriage to Lyonel links the Penroses to the future inheritance of Storm's End; their son Ormund is the next generation of that line.
