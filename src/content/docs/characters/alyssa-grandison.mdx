**Lady Alyssa Grandison** is the daughter of [Ser Steffon Grandison](/characters/steffon-grandison/) and [Lady Maris Grandison](/characters/maris-grandison/) and elder sister of [Orryn Grandison](/characters/orryn-grandison/). She is betrothed to Ser Edric Wagstaff, heir to Greenward.

## Appearance and character

### Appearance

Alyssa has golden-blonde hair, grey-green eyes, freckles, a strong riding build and a slightly crooked smile.

### Character

Alyssa is intelligent, physically bold and curious about life beyond Grandview. She has firm opinions and little patience for people she considers slower than herself. She is loyal to Orryn, though her certainty about what is best for him can leave little room for his own wishes.

## Family

Alyssa grew up with her younger brother Orryn near Grandview. Her betrothal to Ser Edric Wagstaff joins the family to a long-serving knightly house whose lands and castellanship are associated with Grandview's approaches.

## History

Alyssa spent her youth on her parents' lands near Grandview, where she developed a close relationship with her younger brother Orryn. Both shared the golden hair and freckles of their family and enjoyed riding, though Alyssa was more confident in company and more willing to tease her brother about his prospects.

At the time of her cousin Elenei's wedding, Alyssa was betrothed to Ser Edric Wagstaff of Greenward, the heir to one of Grandview's longstanding knightly families. She was among the relatives preparing for the celebration and took an interest in Orryn's coming departure for the Meadows household. She reminded him that their father and Lord Alaric Meadows were cousins, a kinship that carried obligations as well as the prospect of a place in another lord's service.
