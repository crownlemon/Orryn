**Lord Rycherd Rowan** is the ruling lord of Goldengrove and head of [House Rowan](/houses/rowan/) in the Orryn chronology. He holds the hereditary dignity of Marshal of the Northmarch. A skilled negotiator, he is proud of his family's position and attentive to the obligations, privileges and precedence of landed lordship.

## Character

Rycherd is an experienced lord, knowledgeable about the duties of landed families and adept at bargaining. His household expects him to uphold the rights of Goldengrove and to preserve the friendships and obligations of the lesser nobility beneath him. He is demanding of precedence and careful of reputation. Those who offend his standing may find that he treats the affront as a matter of politics rather than a private slight.

His pride is also a weakness. He resents outside interference, remembers slights and is reluctant to compromise publicly. He sees himself as a guardian of the feudal order, yet his sensitivity to insult can damage the relationships that sustain it. His outward conduct should not be mistaken for a single, predictable motive: personal dignity, customary jurisdiction and practical negotiation all shape his decisions.

No definitive physical description has yet been established in the source records.

## Family

Rycherd married **Lady Clarice Hightower**, daughter of Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen, around 180 AC. Clarice's royal ancestry connects their children to Prince Daemon Targaryen. Her younger sister Lady Alysanne was married to Ser Roland Penrose of Parchments, giving the Rowans close kinship ties to House Penrose as well as to Oldtown.

The couple's named surviving children are **Ser Triston Rowan**, aged 23 and heir to Goldengrove, and **Lady Roslin Rowan**, aged 17. In 208 AC, Triston became betrothed to Lady Joy Penrose by letters sealed shortly before Chapter 60. The betrothal is established, but the wedding has not taken place in the recorded chronology. **Ser Nyles Rowan** is Rycherd's younger brother. Other children of the lord and lady are not yet established.

## History

Rycherd rules a substantial northern Reach lordship. The hereditary Marshalship of the Northmarch, held by his family since the post-Dance settlement, predates the present Highgarden court and is not a favour granted by Ser Moryn Tyrell. His early life and the exact circumstances of his accession are not yet described in the available records.

He maintains a close but independent relationship with Ser Moryn, who cultivates the friendship of old landed families. Rycherd also has his own connections to the Hightowers and the Stormlands, and pursues marriage alliances of lasting value to Goldengrove. The ancient rivalry between the Rowans and the Redwynes informs the political life of the Reach without requiring hostility in every individual matter.

During 208 AC, the betrothal of his heir to Lady Joy Penrose expanded the family's dynastic prospects. Lord Harys Oakheart has also been held in his keeping at Goldengrove, bringing the house into discussions around a possible marriage involving Lady Elyce Meadows. No unapproved marriage outcome is assumed.

Rycherd's grievance concerning **[the Cress Hill affair](/events/cress-hill/)** arose after Highgarden's Justiciar heard a dispute involving two of his vassals and Ser Tom Costen drew a knife in the hall. For the lord of Goldengrove, the case concerns both the punishment of the knight and the recognition of customary lordly authority. The matter remains unresolved in the recorded events represented by this article.
