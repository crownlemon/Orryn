## Biography

Lord Rycherd Rowan is the ruling lord of Goldengrove, a major seat in the Reach. His ancient family owes fealty to House Tyrell.

This **Rycherd Rowan is a game-continuity character**. His individual biography is not presented as an established book-canon account.

## Character and conduct

Rycherd is proud and a skilled bargainer. He remembers slights and regards insults to his standing as political questions. His sensitivity to status makes questions of jurisdiction unusually consequential.

## Family and dynastic interests

His household includes Lady Clarice, Triston and Roslin Rowan. Political discussions around Roslin's possible marriage involve Harlan Tyrell and House Blackbar. The outcome is **not established** in this entry.

## Political record

### [The Cress Hill affair](/events/cress-hill/)

Highgarden's Justiciar adjudicated a dispute between two of Rycherd's vassals. Ser Tom Costen drew a knife during proceedings and his fate is in Highgarden's hands. Rycherd regards the intervention as a challenge to his standing.

### Relations with House Meadows

Disputes over demands for grain and spears complicate relations with Alaric Meadows. The particulars and sequence of events require verification against the complete chapters.
