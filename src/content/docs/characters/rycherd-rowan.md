**Lord Rycherd Rowan** is the Lord of Goldengrove, head of [House Rowan](/houses/rowan/) and hereditary Marshal of the Northmarch. An influential lord of the northern Reach, Rycherd is an accomplished bargainer and a defender of the customary privileges of his house.

## Character

Rycherd is an experienced lord, knowledgeable about landed obligations and skilled in negotiation. He maintains friendships and reciprocal duties among the lesser houses of his region and demands respect for the precedence of Goldengrove. An affront to his standing is seldom forgotten and may become a matter of lordly consequence rather than a passing personal quarrel.

His pride can work against his political judgment. He dislikes outside interference, resists public compromise and is quick to defend the jurisdiction of his own house. Yet he understands that lordship rests not only on rank, but also upon bargains, mutual obligations and the loyalty of neighbouring families.

## Family

Rycherd married **Lady Clarice Hightower**, daughter of Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen, around 180 AC. Through Clarice, his children descend from Prince Daemon Targaryen. Her younger sister, Lady Alysanne Hightower, married Ser Roland Penrose of Parchments, connecting Goldengrove closely with the Penroses.

Rycherd and Clarice have two named surviving children: **Ser Triston Rowan**, heir to Goldengrove, and **Lady Roslin Rowan**. In 208 AC, Triston was betrothed to Lady Joy Penrose, daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen. **Ser Nyles Rowan**, Rycherd's younger brother, is also a member of the family.

## History

Rycherd inherited a northern Reach lordship whose power was reinforced by the hereditary Marshalship of the Northmarch. That dignity had belonged to the Rowans since the settlement following the Dance of the Dragons, when it passed from House Osgrey.

He is close to **Ser Moryn Tyrell**, who cultivates the goodwill of the old landed families, but the connection does not bind Goldengrove to all of Moryn's interests. Rycherd maintains independent ties to the Hightowers and the Stormlands, and the old rivalry between House Rowan and House Redwyne continues to influence his dealings with Highgarden.

In 208 AC, the betrothal of his son and heir to Lady Joy Penrose strengthened Goldengrove's dynastic position. Rycherd also held the young Lord Harys Oakheart in his keeping, and became involved in discussions concerning the boy's possible marriage to Lady Elyce Meadows.

The **[Cress Hill affair](/events/cress-hill/)** brought his authority into more direct conflict with that of Highgarden. When the Lord Justiciar judged a dispute involving two Rowan vassals, Ser Tom Costen drew steel. Rycherd asked that Costen be sent back to his own lord's jurisdiction to answer for the knife before those involved in the original quarrel.
