**Ser Olyvar Tyrell** is the youngest son of Lord Leo Tyrell of Highgarden, and the husband of Lady Lenora Hewett of Oakenshield. He is known for his prowess in arms and his popularity among younger knights.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="person:olyvar-tyrell" />

## At Highgarden

Olyvar is an accomplished and charismatic knight, particularly at ease in the lists and training yard. His circle includes tournament companions, household warriors and men connected to maritime and marcher families.

He shares his father's taste for chivalric display, but his influence is not an independent military command. A knight who enjoys his company or a house connected by marriage is not thereby bound to follow his decisions. Ser Denys Tarly is among the young knights who attend him.

## Marriage and maritime ties

His wife Lady Lenora is the daughter of Lord Ormund Hewett of Oakenshield, Lord Admiral of the Reach by Leo's appointment. Her family's concerns with ships and coastal defence connect Olyvar to the Shield Islands. These interests may not always coincide with the priorities of the Redwynes of the Arbor or the marcher lords.
