Lady Alysanne Penrose, born Hightower, is the widow of Ser Roland Penrose and the senior resident member of the family at Parchments in 208 AC.

## Family

She is the daughter of Garmund Hightower and Princess Rhaena Targaryen, and the sister of [Lady Clarice Rowan](/characters/clarice-rowan/). Her three children are Lady Aelinor, Ser Jon and Ser Addam.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree personId="person:alysanne-penrose" />

## History

With Lord Ronnel serving at the royal court, Alysanne remains at the ancestral seat. Her sons Jon and Addam are recorded among the household knights. This household position should not be confused with possession of the hereditary lordship.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1152 and 1161–1163.*
