**Ser Moryn Tyrell** is the second son of Lord Leo Tyrell of Highgarden. Married to Lady Melessa Ashford, he has cultivated relationships with the older landed nobility of the Reach.

## Family

### Marriage and household

Lady Melessa is the younger sister of the reigning Lord Ashford. In 208 AC, the young Matthos Meadows, heir to Grassfield Keep, enters Moryn's service as a squire. Will Risley also serves him as a squire at Highgarden.

## History

### Political standing

Moryn is a perceptive negotiator, skilled in patronage and attentive to the concerns of powerful noble houses. The Rowans of Goldengrove, the Peakes and the Ashford connection through his wife are prominent within his circle of acquaintance.

His influence is personal rather than a separate institution of government. House Rowan, for example, possesses its own interests and inherited privileges; its dealings with Moryn do not confer control of its obligations upon him. Moryn's rivalry with his elder brother Ser Garth is tempered by the fact that Garth remains the lawful heir.
