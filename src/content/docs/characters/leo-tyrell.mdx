**Lord Leo Tyrell**, called **the Longthorn**, is Lord of Highgarden and head of [House Tyrell](/houses/tyrell/), the ruling dynasty of the Reach during the reign of Daeron II. A distinguished jouster and knight, he commands enormous personal prestige among the chivalry of his lands.

## Appearance and character

Leo is a slender older man with silver hair and a grey beard, retaining the carriage of a knight. He is celebrated for courage, an open hand and his love of feasts and tournaments. His welcome at Highgarden and his mastery of ceremony help maintain relationships among houses of differing wealth, history and ambition.

Yet Leo's preference for hospitality and reconciliation is often more effective at preserving outward peace than settling underlying disputes. He entrusts accounts and routine government to his officers and adult sons while remaining unwilling to surrender decisive authority.

## Family

Leo's wife is **Lady Arlanna Peake**, sister of Lord Gormon Peake of Starpike. Their five living children are Garth, Moryn, Ceryse, Olyvar and Brenna. Ser Garth is Leo's heir, followed by his son Ser Harlen in the expected succession.

## History

### The First Blackfyre Rebellion

In 196 AC Leo fought for King Daeron II. He won victories in the Reach but did not bring his forces to the Redgrass Field in time. Highgarden's subsequent settlement involved both rewards and penalties for its bannermen, and memories of the rebellion continue to shape the court.
