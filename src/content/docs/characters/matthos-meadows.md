**Matthos Meadows** is the son and heir of [Lord Alaric Meadows](/characters/alaric-meadows/) and [Lady Jeyne Meadows](/characters/jeyne-meadows/). In 208 AC he entered the service of Ser Moryn Tyrell as a squire at Highgarden.

## Appearance

Matthos is tall for his age, with light brown hair, hazel-green eyes and a sturdy build. He spends much of his time riding and outdoors.

## Character

Courageous, energetic and often popular among boys of his age, Matthos can also be a bully and a poor loser. He boasts, has a talent for blaming others when a contest turns against him, and reacts badly to being corrected.

## Family

Matthos's sisters are [Leonette](/characters/leonette-meadows/) and [Elyce](/characters/elyce-meadows/). His parents retain responsibility for his future and marriage while Ser Moryn's household directs his daily service and training.

## History

Matthos became Ser Moryn Tyrell's squire during a visit to Highgarden in 208 AC. His new position took him away from the resident squires at Grassfield. He received riding instruction at Highgarden and visited Goldengrove with Ser Moryn, where a scuffle with a larger Rowan page left him with a split lip.
