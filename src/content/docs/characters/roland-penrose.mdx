Ser Roland Penrose was the younger brother of [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/). He was married to Lady Alysanne Hightower.

## Family

Roland and Alysanne had three recorded children: Lady Aelinor, Ser Jon and Ser Addam. Their children's descent from House Targaryen comes through Alysanne, whose mother was Princess Rhaena Targaryen.

## History

Roland was deceased by the 208 AC household snapshot. His widow is the senior resident member of the family at Parchments; the exact date and circumstances of his death remain unrecorded in the current dataset.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1152 and 1161.*
