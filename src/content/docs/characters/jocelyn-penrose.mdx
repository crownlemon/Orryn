Lady Jocelyn Penrose is a daughter of [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/) and Princess Elaena Targaryen.

## Family

Her brother Robin is the heir to Parchments. Her sisters are [Lady Laena Baratheon](/characters/laena-baratheon/) and Lady Joy Penrose. Through her mother she belongs to the royal Targaryen family as well as the Penrose dynasty.

## History

The 208 AC household register identifies Jocelyn among the visiting members of House Penrose. A settled marriage and its date are not established by the current structured record.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1151 and 1169.*
