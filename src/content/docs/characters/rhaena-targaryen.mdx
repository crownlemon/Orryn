Princess Rhaena Targaryen is the wife of Garmund Hightower in the documented genealogy.

## Family

She is the daughter of Prince Daemon and Lady Laena Velaryon. Her six daughters include Aelora, Rosamund, Clarice and Alysanne.

## History

Her descendants include members of House Rowan and the collateral Penrose branch.
