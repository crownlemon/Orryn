Robin Penrose is the son and recorded heir of [Lord Ronnel Penrose](/characters/ronnel-penrose/) of [Parchments](/houses/penrose/) and Princess Elaena Targaryen.

## Family

His sisters are Lady Laena, Lady Jocelyn and Lady Joy. His paternal uncle was Ser Roland Penrose, whose children form a separate branch of the house.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree personId="person:robin-penrose" />

## History

Robin is recorded as a visiting member of the Parchments family in 208 AC. His father's court service separates the lord's immediate family from the senior resident branch at Parchments. No date of succession has been established.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, lines 1149–1152 and 1168.*
