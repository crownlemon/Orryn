**Lord Edric Baratheon** is the Lord of Storm's End and head of [House Baratheon](/houses/baratheon/), the ruling house of the Stormlands. He presides over an extensive noble household, supported by household knights, officers and retainers.

<KnowledgeInfobox entityId="person:edric-baratheon" />

## Appearance and character

Edric is a broad-shouldered man with greying black hair, a square beard and dark blue eyes. Age has given him a heavier build without diminishing his commanding presence. He is renowned for courage and direct authority, but his temper is feared by those who have crossed him.

At Storm's End he is known as a generous host to those in his favour. He prefers decisive judgments to the labor of overseeing their execution, which he entrusts to experienced servants and officers.

## Family

He is married to Lady Alysanne, born of House Swann. Their three sons are Ser Lyonel, his heir, Ser Boremund and Ser Gowen. Lyonel has a young son, Ormund, while Boremund's son is Edwyn. Gowen is betrothed to Lady Tya Lannister.
