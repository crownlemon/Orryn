Ser Alyn Merryweather is a household knight at Goldengrove.

## Family

He is a son of Lord Merryweather and Lady Odessa; his siblings are Lymond and Wylla.

<KnowledgeFamilyTree personId="person:alyn-merryweather" />

## History

Lord Alaric Meadows knighted him in 208 AC. He entered Lord Rowan's household by Chapter 60, and is seen in the lists in Chapter 66.

*Sources: Householdbook v12, paragraphs 2316 and 2947; Chapter 66.*
