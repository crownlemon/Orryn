**Ser Garth Tyrell** is the eldest son of Lord Leo Tyrell and heir to [Highgarden](/houses/tyrell/). In 208 AC he is a man of forty, married to Lady Alys Redwyne of the Arbor.

## Family

Garth's wife, Lady Alys, is a daughter of Lord Theomar Redwyne, Master of Ships. Their eldest son, **Ser Harlen Tyrell**, is next in the family's succession after Garth. Lady Alys's younger brother Ser Arron is Lord Seneschal of Highgarden.

## History

### Administration and interests

Garth is regarded as serious and capable in financial and estate matters. His interest in accounts, regular obligations and effective administration often places him among officers whose work is less visible than Highgarden's feasts and tournaments.

His father nevertheless remains the lord and retains final authority. Garth's involvement in governance does not constitute a separate royal or lordly office. His desire for more orderly administration has attracted associates among Redwyne connections and the officers of Highgarden, but those interests do not make every associated house his follower.
