**Ser Raymont Grandison** is the youngest son of the late Lord Gawen Grandison and a household knight in the service of House Baratheon at Storm's End.

## Appearance

Raymont is athletic, with dark brown hair, blue-grey eyes, a broken nose and an easy smile.

## Character

Raymont is charming and capable in company, and his service at Storm's End has brought him useful connections beyond Grandview. He is adept at adapting to new circumstances, but reluctant to remain when an obligation becomes wearisome. His wit can deflect blame as easily as it wins a friend.

## Family

Raymont's elder brothers are [Lord Alester Grandison](/characters/alester-grandison/) and [Ser Steffon Grandison](/characters/steffon-grandison/). His sister [Lady Alys](/characters/alys-musgood/) married into House Musgood. He is uncle to Alester's children Arlan and Elenei and Steffon's children Alyssa and Orryn.

## History

Rather than remain in the administration of Grandview, Raymont entered service at Storm's End. His position preserves a direct Grandison connection to the household of their Baratheon overlords.
