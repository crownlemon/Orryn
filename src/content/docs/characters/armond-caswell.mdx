Lord Armond Caswell is the Lord of Bitterbridge and Defender of the Fords.

## Family

His daughter Jeyne was born of his first marriage. His second wife, Lady Brenna Tyrell, is the mother of his heir Joffrey. Ser Cleyton Caswell is his nephew.

## History

Armond supported Daeron II against Daemon Blackfyre.
