Myranda Webber is a daughter of Ser Wendell Webber, about fifteen in 208 AC.

## Family

Her father serves at Goldengrove; Bryce is one of his sons.

## History

At the feast in Chapter 66 she sits beside Harys Oakheart. Her father's hopes for a match do not establish a betrothal.
