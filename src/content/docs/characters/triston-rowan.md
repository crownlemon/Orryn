**Ser Triston Rowan** is the son and heir of [Lord Rycherd Rowan](/characters/rycherd-rowan/) and [Lady Clarice Rowan](/characters/clarice-rowan/) of Goldengrove. He is betrothed to Lady Joy Penrose, daughter of Lord Ronnel Penrose and Princess Elaena Targaryen.

## Appearance

Triston is handsome and fair-haired, with his father's breadth of shoulder but not his heaviness. At public gatherings he wears Rowan colours and conducts himself with the courtesy expected of a young heir.

## Character

Courteous and mindful of estate affairs, Triston is eager to prove himself a capable lord. He takes an interest in wells, crops, weights and the movement of provisions, though his ambition for greater responsibility occasionally brings him into tension with his father's authority.

## Family

Triston's younger sister is [Lady Roslin Rowan](/characters/roslin-rowan/). His mother's Hightower family descends from Prince Daemon Targaryen. His betrothal to Joy Penrose connects Goldengrove to the household of the Master of Coin and to another royal descendant.

## History

In 208 AC, the Rowan and Penrose families sealed Triston's betrothal shortly before Lady Leonette Meadows's wedding, where it was publicly announced. Joy subsequently came to Goldengrove with her brother Robin. When Lord Alaric Meadows visited Goldengrove, Triston took an interest in the shortage of fodder at Grassfield and assisted with arrangements for the sale of hay and oats. He and Joy were not yet wed.
