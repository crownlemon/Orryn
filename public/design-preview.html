House Rowan

The golden tree of Goldengrove

House Rowan of Goldengrove is an ancient and powerful noble family of the Reach, holding lands near its northern border. In the Orryn chronology, the house is ruled by Lord Rycherd Rowan and owes allegiance to the Tyrells of Highgarden.

History

The family traces its legendary descent to Rowan Gold-Tree and the ancient history of the Reach. Its golden tree upon silver is among the recognizable arms of Westerosi nobility.

The historical record of the house predates the current game by centuries, including its place among the families sworn to the Gardeners and later to the Tyrells. The full chronology will be compiled from referenced ASOIAF source material, rather than copied indiscriminately from a fan wiki.

Vassalage and influence

The Rowans' standing rests upon their ancestral lands and the authority exercised over their sworn families. Precise vassalage in the Orryn year will be reconciled with the worldbook before publication.

Current political affairs

Goldengrove has raised grievances regarding interventions in the affairs of its vassals, particularly following the events at Cress Hill. Matrimonial discussions concerning Roslin Rowan may alter its position among neighbouring powers.

Lord Rycherd Rowan

Lord of Goldengrove

Lord Rycherd Rowan is the ruling lord of House Rowan of Goldengrove. Proud, politically adept and sensitive to matters of rank, he treats any slight to the standing of his house as a matter of lordly consequence.

Appearance and character

Rycherd is a skilled bargainer who remembers slights. He is especially attentive to courtly precedence, and sees an affront to his dignity as something that may affect the authority of Goldengrove itself.

Physical appearance has not yet been established in the available Orryn notes. No portrait is presented as canonical.
